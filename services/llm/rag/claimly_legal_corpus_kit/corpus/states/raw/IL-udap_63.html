(815 ILCS 505/2LLLL)
    Sec. 2LLLL. Violations of the Digital Assets and Consumer Protection Act. Any person who violates Article 5 of the Digital Assets and Consumer Protection Act commits an unlawful practice within the meaning of this Act.
(Source: P.A. 104-428, eff. 8-18-25; 104-852, eff. 8-21-26.)
