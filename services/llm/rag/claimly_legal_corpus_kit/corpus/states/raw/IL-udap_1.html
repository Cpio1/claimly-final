(815 ILCS 505/) (Consumer Fraud and Deceptive Business Practices Act.)
        Title: An Act to protect consumers and borrowers and businessmen against fraud, unfair methods of competition and unfair or deceptive acts or practices in the conduct of any trade or commerce and to give the Attorney General certain powers and duties for the enforcement thereof.
Cite: 815 ILCS 505/1 et seq.
Source: L. 1961, p. 1867. Title amended by P.A. 78-904.
Short title: Consumer Fraud and Deceptive Business Practices Act.
